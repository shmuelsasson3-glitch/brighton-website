What We Install

Full Commercial Installations

From sod to specimen trees - every element of a finished commercial landscape, installed by one crew, held to one standard.

01 - Sodding & Turf

Sodding & Lawn Establishment

We install sod for commercial properties of all sizes - shopping centers, HOAs, office parks, new construction developments. Fresh sod gives you an instant finished lawn with no wait time. We grade, prep the soil, lay and roll, then establish a watering schedule to make sure it takes.

We also handle seeding programs for large open areas where sod isn't practical. Either way, you get a dense, healthy turf that's built to handle foot traffic and look good year after year.

  • Commercial sod installation - all property sizes
  • Soil grading & prep before install
  • Hydroseeding for large open areas
  • Lawn renovation & overseeding
  • Post-install watering & care guidance
02 - Trees & Shrubs

Tree & Shrub Installation

Specimen trees, privacy hedges, ornamental plantings, foundation shrubs - we source and install trees and shrubs for commercial properties across NJ. Every plant is selected for the site conditions, properly balled and burlapped or container-grown, and installed to establish quickly and thrive long-term.

We handle everything from individual specimen trees in front of an office building to full privacy screens and buffer plantings along property lines. Large caliper trees installed with a tree spade. No job too big.

  • Shade, ornamental & evergreen trees
  • Privacy hedges & buffer plantings
  • Foundation & entrance shrub installations
  • Large caliper tree spade service
  • Seasonal flowering shrubs & ornamentals
03 - Landscape Beds

Landscape Bed Installation

Clean, defined beds make or break the look of a commercial property. We install landscape beds from scratch - edging, amended soil, plants, and mulch - or renovate overgrown existing beds back to something sharp. Entrance features, island beds, foundation plantings, perimeter beds along fencing or buildings.

We select plants that work for the site - sun exposure, foot traffic, soil type - and install them to fill in beautifully and require minimal upkeep. A well-designed bed should look intentional, not maintained.

  • New bed creation with edging & soil prep
  • Perennial, annual & seasonal color installs
  • Entrance island & feature beds
  • Mulch installation - all types
  • Bed renovation & replanting
04 - Hardscape

Commercial Hardscape Installs

Walkways, entry plazas, seating areas, curbing, retaining walls - Brighton installs commercial hardscape that's built to hold up under heavy use. We work with pavers, natural stone, concrete, and EP Henry products to create clean, durable surfaces that elevate the entire look of the property.

Commercial hardscape needs to be functional first - proper drainage, ADA compliance, durable materials - and attractive second. We design and build with both in mind.

  • Paver walkways, plazas & entries
  • Concrete curbing & edging
  • Retaining walls - block, stone & timber
  • Outdoor seating areas & gathering spaces
  • ADA-compliant ramps & surfaces
05 - Irrigation

Irrigation System Installation

Every commercial landscape installation we do can be paired with a full irrigation system - properly zoned, timed, and calibrated to keep everything we plant alive and healthy without wasting water or labor. We install systems for turf zones, bed areas, and drip for trees and shrubs.

Smart controllers, rain sensors, and properly sized heads for coverage without runoff. Installed by our team so we know exactly how everything is laid out when it comes time to maintain.

  • Full property irrigation design & install
  • Turf, bed & drip zones
  • Smart controller & rain sensor installation
  • Backflow preventer & compliance
  • Spring startup & winterization programs
