Commercial Site Work

The Heavy Work, Done Right

From a cracked driveway apron to a drainage fix that's been putting off for years — Brighton handles the site work jobs that need to get done right, no matter the size.

No Job Too Small

Most site work companies only want the big contracts. We take pride in the smaller jobs too — the repairs, the fixes, the things that have been ignored. If something on your property needs attention, we'll show up, assess it honestly, and get it handled.

What We Do

Four Services. One Crew.

We self-perform everything — no subs, no handoffs. Our site work crews handle each phase with the same standard we apply to every Brighton project.

01 — Asphalt

Asphalt Paving

Potholes, cracked aprons, faded driveways — asphalt problems start small and get expensive fast if you ignore them. Brighton handles asphalt repairs at the residential and small commercial level, patching and resurfacing before small issues become full replacements.

We also do new installations for driveways and smaller lots, but our bread and butter is the repair work — showing up, doing the job correctly, and leaving the surface better than we found it.

  • Driveway repair & resurfacing
  • Pothole patching & crack filling
  • Apron replacement & transitions
  • Sealcoating & line striping
  • Small lot paving & overlays
02 — Concrete

Concrete Work

A cracked sidewalk panel, a sunken apron, a step that's seen better days — these are exactly the kinds of repairs we do. No job is too small. We come out, remove what's damaged, and pour it back clean. One section or ten, the standard is the same.

Concrete problems only get worse with time and freeze-thaw cycles. Getting it repaired properly the first time is always cheaper than waiting. Our crews work efficiently and leave the site clean.

  • Sidewalk panel repair & replacement
  • Driveway apron & curb repair
  • Steps, stoops & small pads
  • ADA ramp installation
  • Decorative & stamped concrete
03 — Drainage

Drainage

Water pooling in your yard, a wet basement after every storm, a driveway that floods — these are drainage problems, and they don't go away on their own. Brighton finds where the water is going and builds a solution to redirect it away from your home or property for good.

Most drainage fixes are simpler than people think. A properly installed French drain or a regraded swale can solve a problem that's been causing headaches for years. We keep it straightforward, explain what we're doing, and install it to last.

  • French drains & yard inlets
  • Catch basin installation & repair
  • Downspout & surface water redirection
  • Grading & swale correction
  • Dry wells & pop-up emitters
04 — Land Clearing

Land Clearing

Before anything gets built, the site has to be ready. Brighton handles full land clearing for new construction, commercial development, and residential projects — removing trees, brush, stumps, and debris to prepare a clean, grade-ready surface for whatever comes next.

We have the heavy equipment and the crew to clear lots of any size efficiently and safely. Once the land is cleared, we can handle all subsequent site work — grading, drainage, paving — keeping your project moving with one point of contact from raw land to finished site.

  • Tree & brush removal
  • Stump grinding & root removal
  • Debris hauling & disposal
  • Rough grading after clearing
  • Site prep for new construction
05 — Tree Work

Tree Removal & Pruning

Dead trees, overgrown limbs, and hazardous branches are safety risks — and they don't take care of themselves. Brighton handles tree removal and pruning for residential and commercial properties, from a single problem tree to a full property cleanup.

Whether it's a storm-damaged tree that needs to come down or a row of overgrown shrubs that's gotten out of hand, our crew comes out, handles it safely, and hauls everything away. Clean property, no mess left behind.

  • Tree removal & stump grinding
  • Dead limb & hazard removal
  • Crown pruning & shaping
  • Storm damage cleanup
  • Shrub trimming & removal
