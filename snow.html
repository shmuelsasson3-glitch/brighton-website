What We Cover

Every Angle of the Storm

01 - Plowing & Pushing

Plowing & Snow Stacking

Our plow fleet covers parking lots, drives, and access roads - pushing snow to pre-designated stacking areas so your property stays functional and safe throughout the storm. We monitor accumulation in real time and make multiple passes as needed.

Routes are mapped pre-season and crews know every property before the first snowfall. No fumbling in the dark - just efficient, consistent clearing every time it snows.

  • Parking lot & driveway plowing
  • Multi-pass storm coverage
  • Strategic snow stacking
  • Pre-mapped routes for every property
  • Commercial & HOA lots of all sizes
02 - Salting & De-Icing

Salting & De-Icing

Ice is the real liability. Brighton applies road salt and de-icing materials before, during, and after storms to keep surfaces safe and prevent refreeze. We pre-treat when conditions call for it and follow up post-storm to address black ice and compacted snow.

We track temperatures, precipitation type, and forecast windows to apply product at the right time - not too early, not too late - protecting your surfaces and keeping costs efficient.

  • Pre-treatment before major storms
  • Mid-storm salting as needed
  • Post-storm anti-refreeze application
  • Bulk rock salt & liquid de-icer
  • Eco-friendly alternatives available
03 - Sidewalk Clearing

Sidewalk & Walkway Clearing

Over 50 miles of sidewalk cleared every season. Our sidewalk crews follow each plow route, clearing building entrances, paths, ADA ramps, and perimeter walks - then sanding and salting to prevent refreeze and reduce slip-and-fall liability.

Sidewalks are often the last thing a contractor thinks about - we build them into the route from day one. Your tenants and customers get safe access, start to finish.

  • 50+ miles of sidewalk serviced per season
  • Building entrances & ADA ramps
  • Shoveling, snowblowing & hand-salting
  • Perimeter walks & fire lanes
  • Same-pass coverage as plow routes
04 - Pre-Storm Planning

Before Every Storm, We Go Over It With Our Team.

Before the season starts, we physically walk every property with the client - side by side. We're looking at the same parking lots, the same entrances, the same trouble spots. You tell us what matters, we make sure it gets handled.

Then we walk it again with our crew. Every operator who touches that property gets a firsthand look before the first storm. That's how nothing gets missed.

  • In-person walkthrough with every client pre-season
  • Full crew site walks before the season kicks off
  • Every operator knows the property before the storm
  • Priority areas identified & communicated
  • No guesswork - just preparation
